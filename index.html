The Work

Lionsgate is the property. The work is what happens in it.

The common thread: the people who come here leave having said the thing they'd been circling for months.

What Engagements Look Like

Most fall into one of three shapes:

  • An eight-person executive offsite. Two days. A single hard decision. An outside facilitator who keeps the conversation honest.
  • Founder + advisors, strategy session. Half a day of structured conversation, dinner, walk the property, second half the next morning.
  • A senior leader at a transition point. One-on-one work over a weekend — stripping the noise to reach the actual decision.

Cadence

I host roughly eight engagements a year. Privacy and pacing are the product. Inquire early — weekends fill out months in advance.

Background

Twenty years across corporate strategy, global leadership, and entrepreneurial ventures. Senior roles at Novartis leading strategic planning and cross-functional initiatives. MBA, Harvard Business School. BA, Columbia University (summa cum laude, Phi Beta Kappa).

In parallel, two decades curating physical environments designed for reflection and connection. Lionsgate is the current expression of that work.
